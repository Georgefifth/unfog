The problem

Paperwork is written to confuse.

🧾 Medical billsCPT codes, "adjustments", insurance math nobody can verify
🅿️ Tickets & finesescalating penalties buried in legalese
🏠 Lease noticesauto-renewal traps, quiet fee changes
🌍 The burden is unequalhardest on students, immigrants, anyone reading in a second language
The cost isn't confusion.It's missed deadlines, overpaid fees, appeals never filed — money lost to fine print.

The solution

Photo in → clarity and a plan out

Every fact highlights where it sits on the document · legitimacy check · countdowns
What it does

Not a summarizer — a decoder

📖 Plain-language summary + ELI5what it actually says, in words anyone understands
🎯 Grounded factsVL bounding boxes show where each fact lives on the page
⏰ Deadlinescountdowns + one-tap calendar (.ics) export
🛡️ Scam checkflags gift-card demands, arrest threats, fake URLs
✅ Action checklistevery step has a "how do I do this" button
✍️ Drafts that know your factsletters pre-filled with real account numbers & amounts
🎭 Call rehearsalpractice the scary call — by voice — vs an AI rep
💬 Q&A + 🌍 12 languagesanswers quote your document, not generic advice

Challenges → fixes

Open models need babysitting

✗ Pure "!"-token floods from upstream
→ detect mid-stream, reroute to next model
✗ Gated Llama weights, VL capacity exhausted
→ fallback chain: Qwen2.5-72B → Qwen3-32B → Mistral
✗ Models wrap JSON in chatter
→ defensive extraction + retry loop
✗ Blocking HTTP froze the event loop
→ inference moved to threadpool
✗ 25s+ cold starts
→ streaming, timers, honest placeholders
